FORSINING Men's Mechanical Watch

Description:

The Zodiac model celebrates classical astronomy with a functional zodiac disc, dual moonphase, and jumping hour complication. Engraved interpretations on the back case deepen its cosmic storytelling, making it a true horological conversation piece. The watch runs on Titan's in-house Micromotor calibre, engineered for precision and rare complications. Available in Emerald Zodiac and Bronze Zodiac editions, this model is a collector's tribute to the constellations. Key Features: Jumping hour display with minute hand, Functional zodiac disc with engraved back case interpretation, Dual moonphase with lume-filled craters, Sapphire crystal with scratch resistance. Water resistance: 5 ATM. Case size: 42 x 49.55 mm. Thickness: 10.9 mm.
